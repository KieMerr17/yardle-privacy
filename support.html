Reporting a problem

To help us investigate, please include:

  • A description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Your Yardle app version, if known.
  • The puzzle date or number, if relevant.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, where helpful.

Please do not include passwords, authentication tokens or other sensitive information.

Puzzle or photograph not loading?

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen Yardle. If the problem continues, email us with the puzzle date and any message shown on screen.

Results or statistics look incorrect?

Send us the puzzle date, the result you expected and what the app shows. Include a screenshot if possible.

Delete your account and data

In Yardle, open Statistics, then Settings, and select “Delete my account and data”. Review the confirmation before continuing.

This removes your anonymous player account and associated player data. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account from our servers.

If you cannot access this option or need help with a deletion request, email yardle.golf@hotmail.com . We may need your player identifier to locate the correct account.
