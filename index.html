Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Yardle is a daily golf distance game operated by Kieran Merrett (“Yardle”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Kieran Merrett is responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

This policy explains how we handle information when you play Yardle, use its administrator features or contact us.

Players can play without providing a name or email address. Yardle creates an anonymous account to save gameplay and results. We use gameplay activity and, where available, country-level information for our own Yardle Studio statistics. Yardle has no adverts and uses no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell personal information or track users across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

Anonymous player accounts

When you play, Supabase Auth assigns an account identifier, including for anonymous accounts. This identifier connects your gameplay to your player account. Account and session information is stored on your device using the app’s Keychain storage. Ordinary players are not asked to provide an email address.

Playing anonymously does not mean that no personal data is processed. Records associated with a persistent account identifier may still be personal data.

Gameplay content

We store official guesses, scores, Plays Like estimates and round history in Supabase against your player identifier. We use this information to provide results, progress, statistics, streaks and Yardle Handicap.

Play activity and country information

Yardle’s session-statistics features record activity times, guess counts, outcomes and session duration against the player identifier for Yardle Studio analytics.

Where a hosting service supplies a valid two-letter country code, we store that code on the play session. This collection depends on the session-statistics features being enabled in the deployed service. We do not store an IP address or GPS location in these play-session records.

Studio reports provide statistics about use of the game. Exported statistics CSV files do not include raw player identifiers, although the underlying session records remain linked to player accounts.

Administrator information and photographs

Administrators sign in with an email address and password through Supabase Auth. Their authentication session is stored in a separate Keychain on the device.

Administrators can select and upload course photographs to private Supabase Storage. These photographs are associated with the administrator’s puzzles and made available to players for eligible rounds.

Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message contents and any information or attachments you choose to provide.

Service infrastructure

Our hosting and authentication providers process network requests to deliver the service. This can involve technical information such as IP addresses and operational logs, separate from the gameplay and session records described above.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate player and administrator accounts.
  • Provide daily puzzles and validate guesses and results.
  • Save round history, progress, streaks and Yardle Handicap.
  • Apply daily play limits and prevent duplicate submissions or abuse.
  • Provide combined community results in the Clubhouse.
  • Understand game participation and play activity through Yardle Studio statistics.
  • Upload, manage and deliver puzzle photographs.
  • Maintain security and operate the service.
  • Respond to support enquiries and privacy requests.

We do not use this information for advertising, direct marketing or personalised product recommendations.

3. Legal bases for processing

Under UK data protection law, we rely on performance of a contract where processing is necessary to provide the game and features you request.

We rely on legitimate interests for proportionate service statistics, security, abuse prevention, administration and responding to enquiries. These interests are understanding participation in Yardle and maintaining a functioning, secure service, while taking account of users’ rights and interests.

We may also process information to meet legal obligations. Where consent is required for a particular activity, we will request it separately.

4. Service providers and disclosure

We use Supabase for authentication, database hosting and storage of accounts, gameplay, session statistics and puzzle photographs. Supabase processes information to provide these services. See Supabase’s Privacy Policy for information about its practices.

We also use an email provider to receive and respond to messages. Our providers may use infrastructure providers to deliver their services.

We may disclose information where necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request or protect the security and rights of users and the service.

We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. International processing

Service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where applicable, international transfers require appropriate legal protection, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

6. How long we keep information

We retain account and gameplay information while it is needed to provide saved results, history and statistics, unless it is deleted following a valid request.

Session information, support correspondence and operational records are retained only for as long as needed for their stated purposes, taking account of security, dispute resolution and legal requirements. Administrator photographs are retained while needed to provide and manage puzzles.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in backups until those backups expire. Statistics that have been irreversibly anonymised and can no longer identify a player may be retained.

7. Deletion and your rights

To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email yardle.golf@hotmail.com. Removing the app from your device alone does not delete information stored on our servers.

Because ordinary players do not provide a name or email address, we may need your player identifier or other information to locate the correct account and verify a request. If we cannot identify your records, we may be unable to act on them. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive a portable copy of certain information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. Contact us using the email address below to exercise them.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or to your local data protection authority.

8. Device storage and tracking

Yardle stores its own settings and game state using UserDefaults, and authentication information using Keychain storage.

Yardle does not use an advertising identifier, request App Tracking Transparency permission or track users across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not read your contacts.

9. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, when serving the page. This is separate from data collected by the Yardle app. See the GitHub Privacy Statement .

10. Children’s privacy

Ordinary players are not asked to provide a name, email address or other contact details. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can investigate and respond.

11. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information, including authentication, access controls and secure connections. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Yardle or its data practices change. We will publish the current version here and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice of significant changes.

13. Contact

Data controller: Kieran Merrett, operator of Yardle.
